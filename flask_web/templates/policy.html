🛡️ Policy & Privacy

Learn how to use Career&Skills AI safely and responsibly.

Do Not Upload Sensitive Personal Information

  • NRIC / passport number
  • Bank account details
  • Passwords
  • Home address
  • Date of birth
  • Mobile number
  • Private identification numbers

Safe Usage Advice

  1. Remove personal details before uploading your resume.
  2. Keep only career-related information.
  3. Use sample or anonymised data when testing.
  4. Verify AI recommendations before making career decisions.
  5. Escalate complex cases to a human advisor.

System Policy

Career&Skills AI is an educational AI career assistant. It provides resume, career, course, and interview guidance. It does not replace professional career counselling, legal advice, or official employment services.
